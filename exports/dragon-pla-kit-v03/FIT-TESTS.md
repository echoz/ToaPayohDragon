# Print the fit samples first

These 29 sample pieces check the revised v03 joints in their actual print orientations. **Physical PLA fit is still untested.** Digital clearance and slicing checks cannot guarantee the finish or fit from your printer.

Use the X1C's stock 0.4 mm nozzle, the intended PLA and the same print profile as the corresponding final parts. Keep the supplied orientation and **100% scale**. Use each listed colour so the test also represents that filament. Remove brims, strings and support residue carefully; do not sand away the mating dimensions to make a failed test pass. Dry-fit before applying glue.

Start with the generic comparison, then the curved body, supported platform and ladder joints. Complete the other groups before printing their corresponding full parts. All filenames below are under `test-first/<colour>/` and end in `.stl`.

Record the filament, profile and result for each group. A tight, rocking or partly seated sample means that joint needs attention before its full parts are printed. Change the specific fit or a measured slicer compensation and repeat the sample; **do not globally scale the kit or individual mating pieces**. Clearance is intentionally different at different joints. Tiny cropped pieces also cannot reproduce full-length warping or accumulated alignment, so dry-fit complete assemblies before glue.

The tail wall, tail blue insert, tail handrail and threshold samples are full production copies and may be retained for assembly if undamaged. The generic hoop 02 is also an exact production copy, but is printed in cream as a calibration piece. Other crops are fixtures, not substitute model parts.

## Optional assembly cradles — separate from the fit tests

These nine cream pieces are assembly aids, not extra tolerance coupons. Print only the ones needed for the family you are assembling; all print in their supplied flat orientation without supports.

| Cradle filenames | Matching bare cream laminations |
|---|---|
| `test_jig_head_A`, `test_jig_head_B` | Corresponding head outer half below, inner half above. |
| `test_jig_pier_01`, `test_jig_pier_02`, `test_jig_pier_03`, `test_jig_pier_04`, `test_jig_pier_05` | Corresponding pier back half below, front half above. |
| `test_jig_tail_A`, `test_jig_tail_B` | Corresponding tail outer half below, inner half above. |

Use **bare cream halves before fitting colour inserts**. These cradles guide the full outline; their centres are open so raised features such as the head's eye surrounds have clearance underneath. Lay the lower half exterior-down with its flat glue face up, then add its matching upper half with the whole foot key on the correct side. Gently push **both halves toward the cradle's closed foot datum**, check the perimeter against the guide, and align and clamp the exposed outline and flat backs. The side allowance is for positioning, not a press fit. Do not force the parts, put glue on the jig, or use the jig to bend an inaccurate assembly into place. Keep glue out of the cradle and remove it before fitting colour inserts.

The slide2 test shell and left insert are exact full production copies, so a successful pair can be reused. Skip their duplicate production STLs when counting what remains to print. Other full reusable samples are identified in their manifest notes.
