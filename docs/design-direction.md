# Design direction

The target is a cute, recognizable interpretation of the Toa Payoh Dragon Playground. The [approved generated concept](../renders/refinements/sparse-soft-ribs-v2.png) establishes soft proportions, full puffy hoops and wider spacing. It is a style reference, not a render of the model or a dimensioned drawing.

Photographs inform the arrangement of the head, slides, ladder, tail and grounded slide supports. They do not establish measured dimensions. See the [reference records](../references/README.md).

## Current versions

The editable visual master is [v06](../model/blender/dragon-puffy-hoops-v06.blend). Its [nine-angle gallery](../renders/blender-v06/index.html) shows the deeper slide toes and broad supports. That earlier visual model has 18 complete hoops and does not define printable interfaces.

The printable candidate is [v03](../exports/dragon-pla-kit-v03/README.md). It has 15 closed hoops and three open tail rungs, separate colour parts and corrected mechanical interfaces. The [printable assembly render](../exports/dragon-pla-kit-v03/preview/opposite.png) shows this candidate. The public website uses these actual v03 renders.

The [tolerance corrections](tolerance-corrections-v03.md) and family reviews record digital checks. Physical fit, printing and support removal remain pending. Earlier kits and intermediate model revisions are retained in the local authoring archive, outside this public checkout.
